Tiny Colors - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Tiny Colors is a colouring game for young children. It is designed to be safe for families.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Network access

The app does not use the internet. It does not request the network permission and makes no network requests. It contains no links that lead out of the app. (Inside the Android app the game is loaded from the app's own bundled files under an internal web address, so that the device can keep its saved pictures reliably; that address is answered by the app itself and nothing is sent over the network.)

Where your pictures live

Pictures a child colours, the gallery of saved pictures, and the sound and voice settings are kept only on the device, in the app's own storage on the device. Pictures are saved automatically as a child colours. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. If the device is nearly full, the app may automatically remove older unfinished pictures to make room for another unfinished picture, and when you save to the gallery it may remove gallery pictures older than the newest five. Nothing is removed unless that frees enough room for the new save.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access). Spoken colour names use the voice engine already built into the device.

Children

Because the app collects no data of any kind, it collects no personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
